ESS.5.1 · Earth's Systems · ESS.5.1.3

The Gulf Stream is a warm current that flows from south of Florida up the eastern seaboard.

NC objective ESS.5.1.3: Construct an explanation to summarize the ocean's influences on weather and climate in North Carolina.

I can explain how the Atlantic Ocean and the Gulf Stream shape weather and climate on the North Carolina coast.

Big idea

The Gulf Stream is a warm current that flows from south of Florida up the eastern seaboard. It warms the air near the NC coast in cooler months, so the Coastal Plain has milder winters than the mountains. Water and land heat differently, which makes sea breezes by day and land breezes at night. Warm ocean water also fuels hurricanes.

Key ideas
  • The Gulf Stream moderates weather along the East Coast, including NC.
  • NC's Coastal Plain has milder winters than the mountains.
  • Sea breeze: land heats faster in the day, warm air rises, cool air blows in from the sea.
  • Land breeze: at night land cools faster, so air flows from land to sea.
  • Warm ocean water → more clouds and rain, and fuel for hurricanes.

1. Why do NC's coastal towns have milder winters than the mountains?

  1. The ocean and Gulf Stream warm the air
  2. They are closer to the moon
  3. They have more trees
Check answer

A. The ocean and Gulf Stream warm the air: The warm current moderates the air temperature.

2. On a sunny afternoon at the beach, which way does the breeze blow?

  1. From the sea toward the land
  2. From the land toward the sea
Check answer

A. From the sea toward the land: Land heats faster, warm air rises, and cooler sea air moves in.

3. Warmer ocean water usually leads to…

  1. Fewer clouds
  2. More clouds and rain, and stronger hurricanes
Check answer

B. More clouds and rain, and stronger hurricanes: Warm water adds moisture and energy to the air.
