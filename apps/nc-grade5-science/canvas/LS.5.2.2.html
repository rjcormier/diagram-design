LS.5.2 · Ecosystems: Interactions, Energy, and Dynamics · LS.5.2.2

Energy in a food chain starts with the sun.

NC objective LS.5.2.2: Use models to classify organisms within an ecosystem according to the function they serve: producers, consumers, or decomposers.

I can build a food chain with arrows that show energy moving, and sort organisms into producers, consumers and decomposers.

Big idea

Energy in a food chain starts with the sun. Producers (plants) make food. Consumers eat plants or other animals. Decomposers like fungi and bacteria break down dead things and return nutrients to the soil. Arrows point the way the energy moves: from the thing being eaten to the eater.

Key ideas
  • The food of almost any animal can be traced back to plants.
  • Arrows show energy moving from producer toward consumer.
  • Decomposers recycle materials back into the soil for plants to use.
  • A food web is several food chains connected.

Science words

Click a word to see what it means.

producer

Makes its own food using sunlight (plants).

consumer

Gets energy by eating other organisms.

decomposer

Breaks down dead things (fungi, bacteria).

food chain

One path energy takes through an ecosystem.

food web

Many connected food chains.

energy transfer

Energy moving from one organism to another.

model

A diagram or object that explains how something works.

classify

Sort into groups by features.
