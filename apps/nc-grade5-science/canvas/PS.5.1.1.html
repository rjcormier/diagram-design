PS.5.1 · Matter and Its Interactions · PS.5.1.1

When you tear, cut, mix, dissolve, melt or rearrange something in a closed system, the total weight stays the same.

NC objective PS.5.1.1: Carry out investigations to compare the weight of objects before and after an interaction.

I can measure and compare weight before and after something changes, and use the numbers to show that matter is conserved.

Big idea

When you tear, cut, mix, dissolve, melt or rearrange something in a closed system, the total weight stays the same. Matter does not disappear. Sugar stirred into tea seems to vanish, but the scale proves it is still there.

Key ideas
  • The weight of an object equals the weight of all its parts added together.
  • In a closed system, weight before an interaction = weight after.
  • If the weight drops, something left the system (like water vapor or a gas escaping an open cup).
  • Use the right tool (a balance or scale), measure carefully, and do more than one trial.

Science words

Click a word to see what it means.

conservation

Keeping the same total amount. Matter is conserved.

interaction

When things act on each other, like mixing or heating.

closed system

Nothing can get in or out, like a sealed jar.

dissolve

When a solid spreads out in a liquid so you can't see it.

evidence

Data or observations that support an idea.

vanish

To seem to disappear.

property

A trait you can observe or measure, like color or weight.

substance

A kind of matter with its own properties.

Quick check

Answer each question in your head or notebook, then click Check answer.

1. A sealed jar with an ice cube weighs 352.5 g. After the ice melts, what will the jar weigh?

  1. Less than 352.5 g
  2. Exactly 352.5 g
  3. More than 352.5 g
Check answer

B. Exactly 352.5 g: The jar is a closed system. Melting changes the form of the water, not the amount of matter.

2. A sheet of paper weighs 4.8 g. You tear it into 20 pieces and weigh all the pieces together. What do you get?

  1. 4.8 g
  2. Less, because pieces are smaller
  3. 20 × 4.8 g
Check answer

A. 4.8 g: The whole equals the sum of its parts.

3. An open cup of water weighs 250 g on Monday and 212 g on Friday. What is the best explanation?

  1. Matter was destroyed
  2. The scale broke
  3. Water evaporated and left the open cup as water vapor
Check answer

C. Water evaporated and left the open cup as water vapor: The cup is not a closed system. The water became vapor in the air, so 38 g of matter left the cup. It still exists.
