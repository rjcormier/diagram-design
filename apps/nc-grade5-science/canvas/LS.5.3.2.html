LS.5.3 · Heredity: Inheritance and Variation of Traits · LS.5.3.2

Offspring look like their parents because they inherit traits like eye color, petal color and leaf shape.

NC objective LS.5.3.2: Ask questions to compare inherited and acquired traits.

I can tell which traits come from parents (inherited) and which come from the environment or experience (acquired).

Big idea

Offspring look like their parents because they inherit traits like eye color, petal color and leaf shape. Other traits are acquired from the environment: a short haircut, a broken tree limb, or a flamingo turning pink from the food it eats.

Key ideas
  • Inherited traits are passed down through genetic information.
  • Acquired traits come from the environment or experiences.
  • Some traits involve both, like plant height (genes + sunlight and water).
