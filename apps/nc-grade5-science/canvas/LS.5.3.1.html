NC objective LS.5.3.1: Ask questions to compare instincts and learned behaviors.

I can tell the difference between instincts (born knowing) and learned behaviors (practiced or taught).

Big idea

Some behaviors animals are born knowing how to do: spiders spin webs, salmon swim upstream to spawn, you blink. Those are instincts. Others have to be learned: riding a bike, doing pet tricks, speaking a language.

Key ideas
  • Instincts: nest building, spider webs, returning to spawn, sneezing, blinking, pulling away from something hot.
  • Learned: riding a bike, pet tricks, parrots talking, using utensils, tying shoes.
  • Ask questions you can investigate: 'Would a bird raised alone still build a nest?'
