I can read weather graphs to compare places and seasons, and tell weather from climate.

Big idea

Weather is the day-to-day condition of the air: temperature, wind, precipitation, clouds and air pressure. Climate is the typical weather of a place over a long time. In North Carolina, the mountains are cooler than the coast, and every place warms up in summer and cools in winter.

Key ideas
  • Weather tools: thermometer, barometer, wind vane, anemometer, rain gauge.
  • Graph data to find daily and seasonal patterns.
  • Weather = today. Climate = the long-term pattern.
  • Latitude and geography change climate: warmer near the equator, cooler at high elevation.
  • Seasons are opposite in the Northern and Southern Hemispheres.

Science words

Day-to-day condition of the atmosphere.

climate

Typical weather of a place over many years.

precipitation

Water falling from clouds: rain, snow, sleet, hail.

anemometer

Measures wind speed.

wind vane

Shows wind direction.

barometer

Measures air pressure.

rain gauge

Measures how much rain fell.

latitude

How far north or south of the equator.

Talk like a scientist

  • How does wind direction or speed impact weather patterns?

  • Based on the weather data, how would you describe the climate of the location?
